# Case Studies

Real teams using FSMS to build trust, improve productivity, and make data-informed decisions without creating a surveillance culture.

## Luminate Digital — Remote-First Software Agency

**Company:** Luminate Digital
**Industry:** Software development and design consultancy
**Size:** 45 employees (fully remote across 12 time zones)
**Plan:** Growth ($12 per user per month)
**Time with FSMS:** 14 months

### The Challenge

Luminate Digital is a remote-first software agency with team members spread across North America, Europe, and Asia. Before FSMS, the leadership team relied on a combination of status-check meetings, gut feel, and self-reported progress to understand how work was progressing. As the team grew past 30 people, this approach broke down.

"We had no visibility into whether people were actually working or just responding to messages all day," says Sarah Chen, CEO. "Burnout was invisible until someone was already checked out. And our mid-level managers were making resourcing decisions based on who was most vocal, not who had the most bandwidth."

The team evaluated traditional monitoring tools but rejected every one. "We tested one of the big-name tools and our lead designer quit within a week. She said it felt like having a manager stand behind her chair watching every move. That is not the culture we built."

### The Solution

Luminate deployed FSMS on the Growth plan, rolling it out with a company-wide meeting explaining exactly what would be tracked and why. Every employee installed the Agent, linked their Google Calendar for meeting-aware detection, and was given access to their personal dashboard.

"We made two promises: one, every single person can see the same data their manager sees, and two, Private Time is unlimited for the first month so people could test their comfort level. The Private Time usage actually dropped to near zero after week two — once people saw the data was genuinely transparent, they stopped worrying about it."

### Results

- **Before FSMS:** Managers spent an average of 4 hours per week on status-check meetings. Resource allocation decisions were based on perception, not data.
- **After FSMS:** Status-check meetings reduced by 60%. Managers use FSMS trend data in one-on-one coaching conversations instead of asking "what did you do yesterday?"
- **Score confidence:** The team's average productivity score is 78 with a "High Confidence" indicator on 85% of daily scores. The three team members who regularly showed "Uncertain — Mixed Context" flags were discovered to be context-switching across too many projects, leading to a reallocation of work.
- **Adoption:** 42 of 45 employees rated the FSMS rollout as "positive" or "neutral" in a pulse survey. The three who rated it "negative" were all given additional Private Time allowance and opted in after a trial period.

### Key Takeaway

"For agencies, your people are your product. If you treat them like assets to be monitored, you get asset-level output. FSMS let us treat them like professionals who deserve transparent feedback. The productivity data is a bonus — the real win is the trust it builds."

— Sarah Chen, CEO

## Helios Consulting — Professional Services Firm

**Company:** Helios Consulting
**Industry:** Management consulting
**Size:** 120 employees (hybrid: 60% in-office, 40% remote)
**Plan:** Enterprise ($24 per user per month)
**Time with FSMS:** 8 months

### The Challenge

Helios Consulting is a professional services firm with a mix of in-office and remote employees. The compliance team needed to demonstrate that billable hours tracking aligned with actual work activity, but the existing time-tracking system relied entirely on self-reported entries.

"Our compliance framework required us to have reasonable visibility into whether time entries reflected real work," says Marcus Okonkwo, Head of Compliance. "But the partners were resistant to anything that felt like surveillance. They wanted proof, not tools."

The firm operated in a regulated environment where client audits could request evidence of work activity. Self-reported time entries were increasingly being challenged by sophisticated clients who wanted independent verification.

### The Solution

Helios deployed FSMS on the Enterprise plan with identifiable team benchmarking enabled (with employee consent) and configurable screenshot intervals set to 30 minutes. The deployment was phased: the compliance and legal team first, followed by a pilot group of 20 consultants, then a full rollout.

"We used the guided setup wizard, which was critical for a non-technical admin to get everything configured. The jurisdiction-aware notice engine was a lifesaver — we operate in three different states and the EU, and having the right policy template automatically selected for each employee's jurisdiction saved us weeks of legal review."

### Results

- **Compliance confidence:** During a client audit, Helios was able to demonstrate work activity data correlated with billable entries for 95% of the disputed period. The client accepted the evidence.
- **Dispute resolution:** 3 disputes were filed in the first 2 months. All 3 were resolved within the 5-business-day SLA. 2 were upheld (the employees had been miscategorized due to custom internal tooling that FSMS had classified as "neutral" rather than "productive"), and their scores were retroactively corrected.
- **Benchmarking:** Team leads now use the benchmarking data to identify overloaded teams. The consulting division reduced overtime by 18% after identifying that one team was consistently working at 140% of the company average.
- **Policy acknowledgment:** 100% of employees have current, documented policy acknowledgment on file — a critical compliance requirement for their jurisdiction.

### Key Takeaway

"FSMS gave us the audit trail we needed without the surveillance culture we feared. The key was the phased rollout and the transparency features — our employees could see exactly what we could see, and that made all the difference."

— Marcus Okonkwo, Head of Compliance

## Northgate Retail — Operations-Focused Business

**Company:** Northgate Retail
**Industry:** Retail operations (multiple locations)
**Size:** 180 employees (distributed across 5 locations + remote corporate team)
**Plan:** Enterprise ($24 per user per month)
**Time with FSMS:** 6 months

### The Challenge

Northgate Retail operates 5 physical locations plus a corporate office with remote team members. The operations director needed visibility into how time was being spent across the organization — specifically, whether the corporate team's time investment in various operational projects was producing results.

"We had no real data on what our corporate team was doing day to day," says James Park, Director of Operations. "We knew which stores were performing, but we had no idea whether the corporate support time was going to the highest-impact activities."

### The Solution

Northgate deployed FSMS on the Enterprise plan with aggregate team benchmarking. The deployment covered only the corporate team (45 users) rather than store-level staff, focusing on project management, supply chain, and marketing functions.

"We limited FSMS to the roles where activity data would actually inform decisions. Store staff were excluded entirely because their productivity is measured by sales, not screen time. This targeted approach made the rollout much simpler and avoided the surveillance concerns that a company-wide deployment would have raised."

### Results

- **Time allocation insight:** The benchmarking data revealed that the supply chain team was spending 35% of their time in meetings rather than on procurement tasks, leading to a restructuring of meeting schedules.
- **Peak productivity hours:** Analysis of peak productivity hours showed that the marketing team was most productive between 6 AM and 9 AM but most meetings were scheduled at 10 AM. Meeting schedules were adjusted.
- **Tool rationalization:** Activity tracking revealed that the organization was using 47 different SaaS applications, many with overlapping functionality. The company consolidated to 31 tools, saving $28,000 annually in license costs.

### Key Takeaway

"FSMS is not a surveillance tool — it is an operations tool. We used the data to improve processes and remove friction, not to punish people. When the team saw that the data led to fewer meetings and better tools, they became the biggest advocates for the system."

— James Park, Director of Operations
