# Contact Us

We would love to hear from you. Whether you have a question about FSMS, want to book a demo, or need help with your account, our team is here to help.

Our team is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM UTC.

## Support Channels

| Channel | Availability | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| Email (all plans) | Business hours | Within one business day |
| In-app chat (Growth and Enterprise) | Business hours | Within four hours |
| Phone (Enterprise only) | Business hours | Within one hour |
| Critical issues (Enterprise) | 24/7 | Within one hour |

## Sales Inquiries

Interested in FSMS for your team? We offer personalized walkthroughs for teams of any size. Tell us about your needs and we will set up a call.

Please include your team size and the features you are most interested in.

## Book a Demo

We offer live demonstrations of FSMS for teams evaluating the platform. A typical demo runs thirty minutes and covers the Agent installation process, the manager dashboard, the employee self-service portal, and the compliance and governance controls.

[Start Free Trial](/pricing) or email sales@fsms.dev to book a demo.
