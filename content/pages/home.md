# Fair Screen Monitoring — Built on Trust

Employee monitoring doesn't have to mean surveillance. FSMS is a workforce-analytics platform designed for small-to-medium businesses that need productivity data without eroding trust. We give managers visibility into work patterns while giving employees full transparency into what is tracked, when, and why.

## Monitoring That Works *With* Your Team

Traditional monitoring tools create a culture of suspicion. Employees feel watched. Managers get data they cannot act on. FSMS takes a different approach: collect only what is necessary, show everything to everyone, and never operate in the dark.

Our desktop Agent runs visibly on every employee workstation — always identifiable, never stealth. It tracks application usage, URLs, and idle states. Screenshot capture (optional, on Growth and Enterprise plans) happens at fixed intervals and stays redacted until a manager explicitly reviews it. Employees see their own dashboard with the same data their manager sees.

## Built on Three Principles

**Data minimization.** We do not log keystrokes. We do not record continuously. We do not use cameras, microphones, or biometric data. Our activity tracking captures application and window titles at heartbeat intervals — enough to understand work patterns, not enough to invade privacy.

**Employee visibility.** Every employee can see exactly what data FSMS has collected about them, in real time. There is no stealth mode. The Agent icon sits in the system tray with a visible indicator when it is active. Private Time mode lets employees pause all capture with one click — only the duration is recorded.

**Compliance-first design.** FSMS is built for compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the EU AI Act, and relevant US state electronic monitoring statutes. Our audit logging is tamper-evident. Our data retention policies are configurable with automated deletion. We do not perform emotion recognition, biometric inference, or any form of automated decision-making that the AI Act restricts.
