# Features

Three plans, one philosophy. Every tier of FSMS includes core activity tracking, productivity analytics, and employee self-service dashboards. Upgrade for deeper visibility, screenshot monitoring, and advanced administrative controls.

## Starter

Starter is designed for small teams that need lightweight productivity visibility without overcomplicating things. It covers the essentials: what applications and websites your team uses, how time is distributed, and a daily productivity score based on objective activity data.

- **Activity tracking.** Application usage, window titles, and URL visits captured at sixty-second heartbeat intervals. FSMS detects idle states with meeting awareness so legitimate breaks are not penalized.
- **App and URL classification.** Applications and websites are automatically categorized as productive, neutral, or unproductive. Classification rules are customizable per organization.
- **Daily productivity score.** A daily score from zero to one hundred is calculated using a published methodology. Each score includes a confidence indicator so managers understand when data is thin.
- **Employee dashboard.** Every employee sees their own activity timeline, productivity score, and trend chart. No hidden data — full transparency into what FSMS knows about them.
- **Up to ten users.** Starter supports teams of up to ten employees.
- **Email support.** Questions and issues handled via email within one business day.
- **Price: $6 per user per month.**

## Growth

Growth adds visual monitoring and team-level analytics. It is built for growing teams that need more visibility into workflow patterns and the ability to benchmark across the organization.

Everything in Starter, plus:

- **Screenshot monitoring.** Periodic screenshots captured every ten minutes. Screenshots are encrypted in transit and at rest, and they are redacted by default — a manager must actively request to view one. Employees are notified each time a screenshot is taken.
- **Team benchmarking.** Aggregate productivity scores across teams and departments. Benchmarking data is aggregated only — individual scores are not visible to other team members.
- **API access.** REST API with a rate limit of one thousand requests per hour. Build custom integrations, export data, or connect FSMS to your existing tools.
- **Chat support.** In-app chat support with response within four hours during business hours.
- **Up to fifty users.** Growth supports teams of up to fifty employees.
- **Price: $12 per user per month.**

## Enterprise

Enterprise is for organizations that need full configurability, advanced security controls, and dedicated support. It includes all Growth features with higher limits and exclusive administrative capabilities.

Everything in Growth, plus:

- **Configurable screenshot interval.** Screenshot capture frequency is configurable per organization or per team, from every one minute to every sixty minutes.
- **Identifiable team benchmarking.** Benchmarking can optionally show individual scores within a team, with employee consent where required by local regulation.
- **Higher API rate limit.** API access with a rate limit of ten thousand requests per hour.
- **SSO and SAML integration.** Single sign-on via SAML 2.0 compliant providers, including Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace.
- **Custom data retention.** Data retention periods are configurable per data type, with automated deletion enforced at the organization level.
- **Compliance reports.** Automated compliance reports covering data processing, consent records, access logs, and retention enforcement. Exportable in PDF and CSV formats.
- **Dedicated support.** Named account manager and priority support with one-hour response during business hours. Twenty-four-seven support available for critical issues.
- **Unlimited users.** No upper limit on employee count.
- **Price: $24 per user per month.**

## Shared Across All Plans

These capabilities are included at every tier:

- **Real-time dashboard.** Live activity feed visible to managers and employees. Data refreshes every sixty seconds.
- **Offline caching.** The Agent caches activity data locally with AES-256 encryption when connectivity is lost. Data syncs automatically when the connection is restored.
- **Meeting-aware idle detection.** FSMS integrates with Google Calendar to detect scheduled meetings. Idle time during a calendar event is recorded but not penalized in productivity scoring.
- **Data encryption.** All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Screenshots use additional per-file encryption keys.
- **GDPR compliance.** Standard data processing controls, consent management, and Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) workflows included.
