## Plan Details

**Starter — $6 per user per month.** Best for small teams that need essential productivity tracking. Includes activity monitoring, daily scores, employee dashboards, and email support. Supports up to ten users.

**Growth — $12 per user per month.** Best for growing teams that need deeper visibility. Adds screenshot monitoring (ten-minute interval), aggregate team benchmarking, API access (one thousand requests per hour), and in-app chat support. Supports up to fifty users.

**Enterprise — $24 per user per month.** Best for organizations that need full control. Adds configurable screenshot intervals, identifiable benchmarking, ten thousand API requests per hour, SSO and SAML, custom data retention, compliance reports, and dedicated support with twenty-four-seven coverage. Unlimited users.

## Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

**Can I switch plans?** Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. You will be prorated for the remainder of the current cycle.

**Is there an annual billing discount?** Yes, annual billing is available at a discount of two months per year. Annual Starter is $60 per user per year ($5 per user per month). Annual Growth is $120 per user per year ($10 per user per month). Annual Enterprise is $240 per user per year ($20 per user per month).

**What happens if I exceed my user limit?** You will be notified when you reach ninety percent of your plan limit. If you exceed the limit, you will be prompted to upgrade to the next tier or purchase additional user seats. Additional seats beyond the plan limit are not available — you must upgrade to the next plan.

**Do you offer discounts for non-profits or educational institutions?** Yes, we offer a twenty percent discount for verified non-profit organizations and accredited educational institutions. Contact our sales team for details.

**How is billing calculated?** Billing is calculated per active user per month. An active user is any employee who has the FSMS Agent installed and has sent at least one heartbeat in the billing period. Inactive employees (no heartbeat for thirty consecutive days) are not billed.

**Is there a free trial?** Yes, all plans include a fourteen-day free trial with full access to all features of the chosen plan. No credit card is required. At the end of the trial, you will be asked to choose a plan and provide payment information.
